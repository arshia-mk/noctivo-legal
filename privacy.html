Privacy Policy

Your mornings are personal. Here’s how Noctivo handles your information.

Overview

Noctivo is an iPhone alarm and wake-up mission app. This policy describes the current version of Noctivo and this support website. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Noctivo team at deliasupport@gmail.com.

Noctivo does not require an account, use advertising trackers, or sell personal information. Your alarm and mission records are saved locally. Cloud account sync and monetary alarm penalties are not available in this version.

What stays on your device

DataPurpose
Alarms and preferencesAlarm times, repeat days, labels, selected media, mission settings and reminder choices operate your alarms.
Mission referencesSelected reference photos, barcode values and object choices let Noctivo check your configured missions.
Camera and movementLive camera frames, pose estimates, motion and step signals verify exercise and photo missions locally. Camera video is not recorded or uploaded by Noctivo. Captured mission reference photos may be saved locally.
SpeechMicrophone input and recognized words verify Say It On Beat using on-device speech recognition. Noctivo does not upload mission recordings or retain their transcripts as a history.
Imported mediaVideos, thumbnails and sound files you choose are copied into app storage for playback. Noctivo does not upload these files.
Sleep and daily recordsSleep session times, optional sleep audio, wake-up history, journal or feeling entries, saved quotes and companion progress support the features you use.

Noctivo does not continuously collect your precise location. Weather currently uses a city you choose from the app’s available locations.

Permissions you control

iOS asks for access when a feature needs it. Alarm permission enables system alarms; notification permission enables optional reminders; camera, motion, microphone and speech permissions support the relevant missions or optional sleep recordings. Media pickers expose only files or photos you choose.

You can review permissions in iPhone Settings. Removing a permission can stop the feature that needs it. Turning off Noctivo’s reminder switch does not revoke AlarmKit permission or deactivate your alarms.

Network services and disclosures

  • Weather: Noctivo requests forecasts from Open-Meteo using the coordinates of your selected city. The service receives those coordinates and normal connection information such as your IP address. See Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information.
  • Apple services: Apple provides app distribution, TestFlight, system alarms, permission dialogs and App Store purchase management. Apple may process account, purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you voluntarily include. We use these to answer your request and investigate issues. Email is handled through our email provider.
  • Website: GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives normal website request information, including IP addresses. This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in forms or cookies added by Noctivo. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Sharing and backup: If you export records or share content, the destination you choose receives it. iOS may include local app data in device backups according to your Apple settings.

We do not send your mission camera images, speech input, journal, sleep recordings or imported videos to a Noctivo server. We may disclose support correspondence when necessary to comply with law, protect rights or respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them through Noctivo’s controls, erase the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Imported media and optional sleep audio occupy app storage. “Delete Noctivo Data” erases the app’s records and imported media from this iPhone; armed alarms and active sessions must be completed or deactivated first.

Erasing local data does not remove copies you already exported, email correspondence, Apple purchase records or device backups managed outside Noctivo. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription.

We retain support messages only as long as needed to resolve the request, maintain a necessary support record or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; we may need to confirm that you control the email address.

Your choices and rights

You can edit local preferences, remove mission references while editing a deactivated alarm, turn off optional reminders, export written records and delete local data. The current written-record export does not include sleep audio, imported media or mission reference photos.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of personal information we hold, object to processing, request portability, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact deliasupport@gmail.com to exercise these rights. We cannot retrieve local device records unless you choose to send them.

Security and children

Noctivo uses iOS app storage and local processing. No system is completely secure; protect your iPhone and any exported backups. Avoid including sensitive documents, financial information or private content in mission photos or support attachments.

Noctivo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Policy updates

We will update this page when features or data practices change and revise the date below the title. Material changes may also be explained in the app. A future cloud-sync or payment feature will require updated disclosures before it is offered.
