Use missions safely

Exercise, movement, camera, object and speech missions are wake-up prompts, not medical, fitness, security or professional advice. Choose missions and repetition counts appropriate for your abilities and surroundings. Do not perform a mission while driving, near traffic, on unsafe surfaces or where movement could cause injury. Stop exercising if you feel pain or unwell.

Camera and speech verification may make mistakes or be unavailable in poor lighting, noisy surroundings or unsupported device conditions. A photo labeled “Take Pills” verifies a visual task only; it does not establish that medication was taken, is prescribed or is safe. Noctivo does not diagnose conditions or replace medical guidance.
